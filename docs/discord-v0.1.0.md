## Schwung Cam v0.1.0: film your Move with Schwung's own audio

**An Android camera app for Move videos.** It films with any lens on your phone and records the Move's audio **straight from Schwung over WiFi**, not through the mic. Press record, play, press stop, and you get an MP4 ready for YouTube.

**Sync is automatic.** No clap, no slate. The app measures the WiFi delay and the drift between the Move's clock and the phone's, then lines every video frame up with the Move's audio. If WiFi drops some audio, the gap is filled with silence so the sync still holds on a long set.

**The Move's screen in the video.** Turn it on, drag it where you want and pinch to size it. Make black or white pixels see-through so only the lit pixels float over the picture.

**Mastered after every take:** −14 LUFS for YouTube (or −9), a glue compressor and a −1 dBTP true-peak limiter. A 24-minute take saves in about 30 s on a Pixel, and keeps saving if you leave the app.

**Stems.** With LINK on in movy, pick any of its 16 tracks, sends or master and each take also saves them as 24-bit WAVs that start at the video's first frame. Drop them in a DAW at 0:00 and they line up with the picture.

**Takes viewer:** your takes by day, waveforms, stem solo/mute, a drawn Move that plays your pads, knobs and screen along with the video, and share or delete any part of a take (with Undo).

**Also:** 720p/1080p/4K, 16:9 or 9:16 upright, exposure lock, raw WAV, and an AI timeline zip of every gesture you can give an AI to caption the video.

**Needs:** Android 10+ (64-bit), the phone and Move on the same WiFi, and **Mirror Display** on in Schwung's settings.
